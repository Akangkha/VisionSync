## ✨ What is Vision Sync

* Vision Sync delivers **separate, synchronized visual stimuli** to each eye — promoting binocular coordination and visual fusion.
* Designed for patients (especially children) undergoing vision rehabilitation or therapy.
* Delivers **game-like visual exercises**, turning therapy into an engaging, accessible experience.
* Tracks progress over time, enabling both users and clinicians to monitor improvements in eye coordination and stimulus response.

---

## 🧠 Motivation & Why It Matters

Amblyopia — commonly known as “lazy eye” — affects many children and adults. Traditional therapy often requires visits to a clinic and can be tedious or discouraging for young patients.

Vision Sync aims to:

* Make vision therapy **accessible from home** — reducing dependence on clinic visits.
* **Gamify** therapy exercises to increase compliance and motivation, especially for younger patients.
* Use modern web / VR technologies to deliver **structured, repeatable, and trackable therapy sessions**.



## 🎯 Features

* **Binocular stimulus synchronization** — different visuals for left and right eye, synchronized in time for fusion training.
* **Interactive & gamified therapy sessions**, making exercises engaging for children and adults.
* **History and progress tracking** — users/therapists can view therapy logs and improvement over time.
* **Web-based, cross-platform** — accessible via browsers; VR-enabled for immersive training.
*  **Custom session generation** — therapists or parents can configure stimulus types, durations, and difficulty levels.
* (Future) **User profiles, progress analytics, and reporting** for clinical use.

---


## 🎯 Who It’s For

* Patients with **amblyopia (lazy eye)** — children or adults under vision therapy.
* **Optometrists / ophthalmologists / vision therapists** who want a digital, home-accessible supplement to traditional therapy.
* **Parents / caregivers** seeking engaging therapy tools for their children.
* Developers or researchers interested in **digital therapeutics, VR/AR, vision rehabilitation, or gamified therapy apps**.
